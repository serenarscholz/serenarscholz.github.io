Hi there! I'm a PhD candidate in the Dept. of Earth & Planetary Sciences at Yale University, advised by [Prof. Juan Lora](https://earth.yale.edu/profile/juan-m-lora). 

I primarily study atmospheric rivers: long, narrow storm systems that transport water and energy across the globe. I use a combination of observations and models, with a particular focus on idealized modeling and model hierarchies. I'm also interested in extratropical cyclones and storm track dynamics, Earth's past and future (hydro)climate, the influence of land on weather and rainfall, and the global water and energy cycles.
