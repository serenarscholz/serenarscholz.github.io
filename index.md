Hi there! I'm a PhD candidate in Earth & Planetary Sciences at Yale University. I am advised by [Prof. Juan Lora](https://earth.yale.edu/profile/juan-m-lora) in the [Atmosphere, Ocean & Climate Dynamics](https://earth.yale.edu/research/atmosphere-oceans-climate-dynamics) division.

My dissertation research focuses on atmospheric rivers: long, narrow storm systems that transport water and energy across the globe. I study these systems using combination of observations and models of varying complexity.
<br>
<br>
I'm also interested in extratropical cyclones and storm track dynamics, Earth's past and future (hydro)climate, the influence of land on weather and rainfall, and the global water and energy cycles.
