Hi there! I'm a PhD candidate in Earth & Planetary Sciences at Yale University. I am advised by [Prof. Juan Lora](https://earth.yale.edu/profile/juan-m-lora) in the [Atmosphere, Ocean & Climate Dynamics](https://earth.yale.edu/research/atmosphere-oceans-climate-dynamics) division.

My dissertation research focuses on [atmospheric rivers](https://doi.org/10.1063/pt.171e3ee933): long, narrow storm systems that transport water and energy across the globe. I use a combination of observations and models spanning a wide range of complexity (who doesn't love a good model hierarchy!).
<br>
<br>
I'm also interested in extratropical cyclones and storm track dynamics, Earth's past and future (hydro)climate, the influence of land on weather systems, and the global water and energy cycles.
