Welcome! I'm a PhD candidate at Yale University, working with Prof. Juan Lora in the [Planetary Atmospheres group](https://earth.yale.edu/profile/juan-m-lora). 

I study atmospheric rivers: long, narrow storm systems that transport water and energy across the globe. I'm also interested in extratropical storm track dynamics and variability, Earth's past and future hydroclimate, and the global water and energy cycles.
