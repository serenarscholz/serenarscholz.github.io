Hi there! I'm a PhD candidate at Yale University, advised by [Prof. Juan Lora](https://earth.yale.edu/profile/juan-m-lora). 

I study atmospheric rivers: long, narrow storm systems that transport water and energy across the globe. I use a combination of observations and models, with a particular focus on idealized modeling using [Isca](https://execlim.github.io/IscaWebsite/). I'm also interested in storm track dynamics, Earth's past and future (hydro)climate, and the global water and energy cycles.
